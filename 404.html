Take-home: audit and fix an inherited implementation

Pinewake Exchange is a fictional listings marketplace. A previous contractor set up its tracking: engineering added a data layer and data-track attributes, and the contractor built a GTM container that sends events to Fullstory. That container is live on this site right now. Every Fullstory call it makes shows in the call log at the bottom right, along with any errors Fullstory would give. The log is kept as you move between pages.

The business isn't confident in the data. We'd like your assessment and a plan to fix it.

What the tracking is supposed to do

NeedDetails
Page propertiesPage name and page type on every page.
Search CompletedKeyword, and the number of results as a number.
Filter AppliedFilter name and value, only when the visitor changes a filter.
Listing ViewedListing ID, make, and price as a number (none when there's no price).
Phone RevealedWhen the dealer phone is revealed on the main listing (not the similar-listings cards), with the listing ID.
Lead SubmittedOnly when the lead form is sent successfully, with the lead reference and listing ID.
PrivacyNo email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details in Fullstory.

Our data-track standard is five lowercase, hyphenated parts separated by pipes: page|section|element|cta-type|cta-value, for example vdp|lead-form|button|conversion|send-email.

What to do

  1. Audit. Review the data layer, trk, the data-track attributes and the inherited GTM container. What works, what's broken, and why?
  2. Fix what you can in GTM. Download the inherited container and import it into your own GTM account (Admin → Import Container). To test your version on this site, open [this page's address]?gtm=GTM-XXXXXXX with your container ID. It stays loaded as you move between pages in that tab. Use the link in the black bar to switch back to the inherited container.
  3. Write the requests for engineering. Some problems can't be fixed well in GTM. Write what you'd ask engineering to change or add to the data layer and the data-track attributes. Write it as you would for a real dev team.

What to send back

  • Your audit, as a short document.
  • Your fixed container (Admin → Export Container, as JSON).
  • Your engineering requests.
  • A short note on the tools you used, including any AI tools, and how you used them.

Please spend no more than 3–4 hours. We'd rather see clear priorities than full coverage. AI tools are allowed. We'll go through your work together in a 30-minute call afterward.
